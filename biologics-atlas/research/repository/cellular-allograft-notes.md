## Rule decisions a reviewer should know
- **On-label in foot/ankle and nonunion.** Following the brief, a 361 graft labeled for "musculoskeletal defects" or "bone void filler" is marked on_label true in fusion, including foot and ankle fusion. The existing trinity-elite record marks ankle and subtalar arthrodesis on_label false. Consolidation should pick one rule.
- **Withdrawn products keep their historical tier** (established), matching the existing map3 record.
- **NuCel and PureGen are set to path none-unapproved**, not 361. Each owner either conceded or was told that 361 does not apply: Organogenesis's FY2024 10-K says a BLA is needed, and FDA sent Parcell an untitled letter in 2011. Their applications are marked investigational and on_label false.
- **Interfyl** is categorized as amniotic (placental matrix). It is not a cellular bone graft and is included as the category's regulatory warning, as the brief asked. The FDA letter and Celularity's 8-K disagree about whether the 2004 RFD was issued for Interfyl itself; both readings are recorded.
- **carmell-bha** is acellular (plasma plus genipin plus beta-TCP, IND only, paused). It is categorized "other" and is included only because the brief named Carmell.
- **The class record grades lumbar PLF evidence I.** That rests on the Buttermann 2026 randomized trial (cellular allograft-MSC arms 62-67% posterior fusion vs 98% iliac crest). Its caveats are recorded: single surgeon, products not named, historical allograft control, and retrospective registration (NCT07187362 lists 119 enrolled; the paper reports 175).
- **The Cigna policy 0118 text** was read through a third-party copy (genhealth.ai). The cigna.com PDF returned HTML. Treat the Cigna wording as secondary.
- **FDA's 2023 TB letter to HCT/P establishments.** The CDC page links to an FDA URL (…hctp-establishments-1) that now serves an unrelated 2024 saline-recall notice. The live FDA TB safety communication (September 12, 2023) is cited instead.

## Scope items not given their own record
- **Surgenex.** The FDA Warning Letter of November 15, 2021 (OBPO 21-615254) covers SurCord, SurForce and SurFactor. These are umbilical cord and amniotic injectables for orthopedic conditions, not bone grafts, and are already covered by the existing umbilical-cord-wharton-jelly-injectables and amniotic-orthopedic-products records. No Surgenex bone product was found.
- **SpineSmith.** Only graft-delivery and containment patents were found, plus a Celling Biosciences delivery-system listing (autologous BMAC, covered by the bmac record). No cellular allograft.
- **Bone Bank Allografts.** It processes Globus ViaCell (insert confirms). No BBA-branded cellular product was found.
- **Medtronic.** No current cellular brand was found. FiberCel (2019-2021) is its only one, and Medtronic told Aziyo in October 2021 it would stop distributing cellular bone products.
- **Stryker beyond Bio4, DePuy Synthes beyond ViviGen, Smith+Nephew/Osiris.** Nothing new was found. "Ovation OS" was Osiris's original name for what became Bio4 (existing record). Osiris's Ovation amniotic matrix appears in Cigna's amniotic list.

- **SeaSpine heritage.** No SeaSpine-branded cellular allograft was found. Orthofix's FY2025 10-K lists only Trinity ELITE and Virtuos Lyograft as cellular. "OsteoSurge" could not be confirmed as a cellular product.
