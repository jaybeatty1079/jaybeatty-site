The BD lens

How a biologic gets paid

Inpatient, the graft is bundled into the MS-DRG and nobody pays for it separately, so the hospital's value analysis committee is the real customer. Outpatient it is packaged into the procedure. The exceptions are the levers: two new-technology add-on payments, one live pass-through code, and payer policies that decide what counts as experimental.

Regulatory lanes

Device, 361 tissue, or 351 biologic

The three lanesWhat each one can and cannot claim

Device (510(k), PMA, De Novo, HDE). Synthetics, DBM putties with a carrier, rhBMP-2, P-15, rhPDGF, and the kits that make PRP or marrow concentrate. Surgeons may use a device off-label; the company may not promote it off-label. Bone void fillers are cleared for voids "not intrinsic to the stability of the bony structure," which is why most fusion use is off-label.

361 human tissue (HCT/P). Structural allograft, DBM without a carrier, and the cellular bone allografts. No premarket review, only registration, provided the tissue is minimally manipulated and used homologously. An osteoinductive or healing claim can tip a product into the next lane.

351 biologic (BLA). A licensed biologic with clinical trials behind it. As of September 2026 the FDA's approved cellular and gene therapy list has no product for bone, fracture or fusion. Every stem cell or exosome product marketed to orthopedic clinics is unapproved.

Methodology

How this was built, and what it will not claim

A reference built to be challenged. Every product line names its source, every tier is explained, and anything that could not be confirmed is marked rather than smoothed over.

SourcesPublic only

  • FDA 510(k), PMA, De Novo and HDE databases (every CDRH number on this page was checked against openFDA), FDA warning and untitled letters, the tissue establishment registry.
  • PubMed and ClinicalTrials.gov for every evidence row. Links go to the primary record.
  • CMS FY2027 IPPS final rule and tables, CY2026 OPPS and physician fee schedule, Medicare contractor LCDs, and the public medical policies of Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare and Blue plans.
  • SEC filings and company press releases for ownership, distribution agreements and revenue. Market-size figures are third-party estimates quoted in public releases and labeled as such.
  • No paywalled market-share data, no sales rep hearsay, no marketing claims copied as findings.

RulesWhat the tiers and badges mean

  • Established means long on the US market in that application. Emerging means cleared, approved or launched in roughly the last three years, or in a pivotal trial. Investigational means not authorized for that use. Within a tier, products are ordered by evidence grade, never by sales.
  • On label is read from the FDA indication text, not the brochure. Most bone void fillers are off-label in fusion.
  • Unverified marks a field that could not be confirmed from a primary source. It is left visible on purpose.
  • Where no US annual procedure count exists, the page says so and shows incidence with its source instead of inventing a number.
  • Fracture classifications are described in words. AO/OTA figures are copyrighted.
