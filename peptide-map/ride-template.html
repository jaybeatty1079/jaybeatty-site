    How to read the ride

    Four windows open on the climb, one per station: the raw source, purifying and testing, fill and finish, dispensing. Green means the rules permit the step, amber means with conditions, red means the gate is closed. A cart that stops at a gate, or gets shunted onto the research-use-only siding, is the answer.

    Where the facts come from

    Every status and date on the plate is a row in the federal register behind this tool: FDA's lists and letters, the Federal Register, the courts. The map holds the register, the 503B facilities and the documented supply routes.

    What it is not

    Not legal advice, and not a statement that any peptide is safe or works. "Tolerated" means FDA or HHS says it does not intend to act while conditions are met. It is never approval.
