The words on the ride

The terms and acronyms a reader meets at the counter, on the track, on the plate and on the map, in plain language. Where a term carries a date or a count, it matches the register behind the tool.

Definitions are the tool's own plain-language summaries of public rules and practice, as of the date above. They are not legal advice. "Tolerated" means FDA or HHS says it does not intend to act while conditions are met; it is never approval.
