Per store
What the store gets
  • Net profit up without selling a car. Payroll tax drops on every enrolled employee, and the difference lands on the P&L.
  • Your people take home more. Only employees who come out ahead are enrolled. More pay for the same work is a retention tool you didn't have to fund.
  • $0 care for employees and their families. Virtual primary care, urgent care, therapy, dermatology and 70-plus generics, with no copay and no claim.
  • Fewer claims on the company health plan. That $0 care is the first stop, so visits and fills that would have hit the company's self-funded or level-funded plan never do.
  • Nothing changes on major medical. This sits alongside the health plan you have. It never replaces it.
  • Nothing is signed on these numbers. A payroll analysis confirms who qualifies and what each store saves before anyone commits.

How it works
  • Employees who enroll elect a pre-tax contribution under a Section 125 plan. It funds $0 virtual primary care, urgent care, therapy, dermatology, 70-plus generics and coaching for them and their dependents.
  • Their taxable wages drop, so each store pays less FICA. After the plan's administration fee, that is the per-employee figure above, and it lands on the P&L as net.
  • When an employee completes qualifying care, a wellness reward comes back on the same paycheck. Only employees who come out ahead are enrolled.
  • The $0 care is the first stop, so some visits and prescriptions never reach the company's self-funded or level-funded plan.
  • It sits alongside major medical and never replaces it. Nothing is signed on these numbers; a payroll analysis confirms who qualifies and what each store saves.

Illustrative only. No specific tax outcome is guaranteed; results depend on a payroll analysis and on current federal and state law.
