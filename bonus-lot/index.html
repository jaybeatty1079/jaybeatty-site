    How it works
    • Employees who enroll elect a pre-tax contribution under a Section 125 plan. It funds $0 virtual primary care, urgent care, therapy, dermatology, 70-plus generics and coaching for them and their dependents.
    • Their taxable wages drop, so each {site} pays less FICA. After the plan's administration fee, that is the per-employee figure above, and it lands on the P&L as net.
    • When an employee completes qualifying care, a wellness reward comes back on the same paycheck. Only employees who come out ahead are enrolled.
    • The $0 care is the first stop, so some visits and prescriptions never reach the company's self-funded or level-funded plan.
    • It sits alongside major medical and never replaces it. Nothing is signed on these numbers; a payroll analysis confirms who qualifies and what each {site} saves.

    Illustrative only. No specific tax outcome is guaranteed; results depend on a payroll analysis and on current federal and state law. Employer figures use the plan's published per-employee math (the monthly pre-tax contribution × 7.65% FICA, less the plan's administration fee) and leave out any workers' compensation, FUTA or SUTA effect. Revenue, margin and pay-plan figures are industry averages or your own entries, not a forecast for your store. Participation is voluntary; employees typically see higher take-home pay, though some do not. This is a fixed indemnity benefit, not health insurance; it sits alongside, not in place of, major medical.
