Programs I represent

Current offerings

Alongside consulting work, I represent three programs directly. Each one has a specific buyer, and I only bring it up when the fit is real.

For business owners, CPAs, payroll firms and PEOs

Section 125 wellness plan

An employer benefits program built on a Section 125 cafeteria plan. Employees get added health and wellness benefits; employers see a reduction in payroll tax on participating employees. Designed by ERISA counsel with an independent opinion letter and audit protection on file.

Results depend on the employer's payroll and participation. I share the compliance file first and let you or your CPA judge it.

For companies with high-earning 1099 producers

Non-qualified deferred compensation

Executive benefit plans for firms whose top producers are independent contractors and locked out of standard retirement vehicles. A vesting deferred structure that helps attract and keep producers, and that a competitor can't copy the way they copy a split.

Common fits: real estate brokerages, insurance agencies, physician groups. The ERISA attorney models the plan; I run the first conversation.

For independent hospital-based physician groups

No Surprises Act revenue recovery

An automated service that takes out-of-network claims through the federal IDR process so physician-owned radiology, anesthesia, emergency and pathology groups collect what they're owed. Paid as a percentage of what's recovered, so the group carries no upfront cost.

No group is promised a specific recovery. We look at your claim mix first. Offered with Advantify.
