Business development

What an engagement delivers

The work of a senior BD leader for companies in an early growth phase or opening a new market: find the buyer, earn the meeting, close, then build the system so it repeats. On contract or as a full-time hire, with a weekly scoreboard your leadership can audit.

Pipeline built from zero

Target mapping, list building and multi-channel outreach by phone, email and LinkedIn, run with CRM discipline and weekly activity reporting. Surgeons, hospital leaders and physician practices, reached through the channels they actually answer.

Channel and partner programs

Distributor networks, referral partnerships and the incentive structures behind them. Independent reps and distributors for devices and diagnostics; CPAs, payroll firms and PEOs for employer programs.

Clinical and economic selling

Fluent with surgeons, service-line leaders, CFOs and payers. Comfortable moving a conversation from the OR to episode-based reimbursement (ACO, CMS TEAM) without losing either audience.

Government and enterprise contracting

State and municipal RFPs, IDN and value-analysis approvals, GPO negotiations and VA contracting. I know what the committee needs to see before it says yes, and how long it really takes.

An operator's view

Six years running a CLIA laboratory as COO: sales, operations, IT, finance, compliance and a $1M-a-month budget. I have been the buyer for instruments, reagents, software and payer deals, so I know what a CFO, a lab director and a supply chain committee need to see before they sign.

Leading leaders

Fourteen years running sales organizations through managers: area managers at Specialized Orthopedic Solutions, direct sales leaders at Olympus Biotech, department heads at ATCG. Territories, quotas, comp plans, the playbook and the weekly forecast review.

ATCG · clinical genetics laboratoryCOO · identity, copy and art direction

Starting in 2019 I ran ATCG as COO, a CLIA high-complexity clinical genetics lab in Irvine, California, selling pharmacogenomic, hereditary cancer, cardiac and carrier screening tests into physician offices and, later, government programs. I designed the lab's identity, including this mark, and wrote and art-directed every piece the field team carried into an office. The collateral portfolio shows the physician and patient brochures, built around the first objection in every practice: "is this more work for my staff?"

Reaching people other labs skipped. One of ATCG's missions was to bring these tests to a broader group of people, so our outreach went to dense and underserved areas. We held Medicaid contracts across the country and learned how to serve that community well: in-field outreach, minute clinics and virtual care.

The carrier screening panel. Our best-received launch. Couples planning a family wanted every piece of information they could get before starting, and the marketing was built around that.

The panel we turned down. We were offered an investigational Alzheimer's, dementia and Parkinson's panel built on genes tied to those conditions. Ownership saw a booming market. We said no: once the result came back, there was nothing a doctor or patient could act on except a clinical trial, and a trial should pay for its own testing, not insurance. The market reached the same conclusion.

The statewide long-term-care rollout. Dozens of facilities had to be onboarded in a tight window and kept compliant with state testing and reporting. I designed the rollout, ran the webinars, had marketing build the instructions and hired the team that got every facility live by the deadline. When another RFP winner lost its end users, the state called me to take over their footprint.

The anesthesia panel. A malignant hyperthermia risk panel for surgical patients, built for anesthesiologists and surgeons. How it was packaged.

About

I started in operating rooms and ended up running a laboratory. The thread through all of it is the same: find the buyer, earn the meeting, close, then build the system so it happens again without me in every room.

I've spent my career in healthcare sales and operations, first selling implants and biologics to orthopedic surgeons, then leading national sales through independent distributors, then running a clinical laboratory as COO through the largest testing program in its history.
