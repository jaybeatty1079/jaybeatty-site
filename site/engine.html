The Reconciliation Engine

One year of Medicare episodes goes in as punched cards. Gears and belts do what the CMS TEAM rules do: build the target price, subtract what the hospital spent, then apply the track floor, the quality score and the stop limits. The payment comes out on the dial.

It runs the same rules as my TEAM episode calculator. Move a control and the belts and springs move with it. Touch any part to see what it is and what it does.
